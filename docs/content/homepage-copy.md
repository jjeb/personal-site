## Problems I Help Solve

### Modernize consequential systems

Legacy systems often carry real business value alongside accumulated constraints. I help teams understand those constraints, define practical boundaries, and move toward architectures they can change with greater confidence.

### Make production systems operable

Reliable software requires more than a successful implementation. I work on readiness, observability, capacity, deployment, failure modes, and the tools teams need to understand what their systems are doing.

### Build tools that make experts more capable

I build languages, workflows, and developer tools that reduce friction around complex work. The goal is not automation for its own sake, but giving engineers and domain experts better ways to express decisions and act on them.

## About Juan

I am a senior software engineer and former engineering director with more than fifteen years of experience across software, infrastructure, data systems, commerce, and engineering leadership.

My career has repeatedly placed me between boundaries: product and production, software and infrastructure, implementation and organizational decision-making. I am most interested in work where technical depth must be connected to how people understand, operate, and improve a system over time.
